WeatherWear Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2, 2026

WeatherWear has no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising. The developer doesn't run a server for the app and receives none of your data.

Your location

With your permission, WeatherWear uses your location while you're using the app. It's sent to Apple to get your local weather through Apple Weather and to look up the name of your area. WeatherWear doesn't save your location. Apple's handling of these requests is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

You can turn off location access at any time in the Settings app on your iPhone.

What stays on your device

Your settings and the outfits you log, including their ratings and the weather at the time, are stored only on your device. You can delete saved outfits in the app. Deleting the app removes everything.

TestFlight

If you test WeatherWear through TestFlight, Apple shares your tester details, crash reports, and any feedback you send with the developer. This is covered by Apple's TestFlight terms.

Children

WeatherWear isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
